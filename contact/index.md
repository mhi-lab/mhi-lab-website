## **OPEN POSITIONS**

For applications and inquiries, please contact Prof. Cheng Chen at [cchen@eee.hku.hk](mailto:cchen@eee.hku.hk).

### **Prospective PhD Student**

- We are looking for self-motivated PhD students who are interested in medical AI. Please drop me an email with your CV, transcripts, 1–2 page research statement (optional).
- There are multiple fully funded options
  - HKU EEE PhD Program (HKD 19,135–19,655 / month)
  - **HKU Presidential PhD Scholarship (HKU-PS)** (HKD 28,700 / month)
  - **Hong Kong PhD Fellowship (HKPF)** (HKD 28,700 / month)
  - HKU-BICI Joint PhD Programme

### **Postdoc/RA/Intern/Visiting Students**

- We welcome applications from motivated postdoctoral researchers, research assistants, interns, and visiting students interested in medical AI and related areas. Please email me your CV, a brief introduction to your research interests and relevant experience, and your preferred position or visiting period.
- If you are an HKU student interested in doing research with our group, also feel free to drop me an email.
